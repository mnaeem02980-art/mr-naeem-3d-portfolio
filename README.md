# PALMO × MR NAEEM — Award-Winning 3D Storytelling Portfolio Experience

Yeh website bilkul video ke mutabiq **MR NAEEM** ke naam se design aur develop ki gayi hai.

## 🌟 Key Features Included (Matching the Video):

1. **Preloader Screen:**
   - Dark chocolate theme `#331D12` with pulsing Golden Palm logo
   - "MR NAEEM - 3D STORYTELLING PORTFOLIO"
   - Progress bar + "00:00" to "00:100" timer reveal.

2. **Hero Section (Interactive 3D Coconut):**
   - Bold typography: **PARADISE IN EVERY SIP.**
   - Real-time WebGL 3D Coconut (Three.js) with fibrous organic texture, light reflection, mouse parallax and 360° drag rotation.
   - Interactive "Crack Open 🥥" button with snap animation.
   - Nutritional callouts with pointers ("Rich in Electrolytes", "No Added Sugar").
   - "GYRO SENSOR • 3D INTERACTIVE READY" live status badge.

3. **Section 2: Inside The Coconut (The Real Source):**
   - 3D Coconut cracked open revealing luscious white coconut meat and translucent refreshing coconut water with animated wave shimmer.
   - Orbiting badges: "100% Natural Hydration", "0 GM Added Sugar", "500 MG Potassium", "Cold Pressed".
   - Dynamic status bar syncing with scroll: "HARVESTING & SHAKING 009" to "CANNED 074".

4. **Section 3: The 3D Product Can Transformation:**
   - 3D Aluminum Can with metallic rim, pull-tab, and custom Palmo label.
   - 360° mouse/touch spin.
   - Live flavor switcher buttons ("Pure Coconut", "Golden Pineapple", "Ruby Watermelon") that instantly morph the can textures!

5. **Section 4: Fresh Banner (Scalloped Edges):**
   - Retro wavy scalloped SVG border dividers.
   - "PURE COCONUT WATER. NATURALLY HYDRATING, REFRESHINGLY FRESH, FROM NATURE'S SOURCE."
   - Smiley face badge and "That's Why People Love To Drink It!" vintage stamp.

6. **Section 5: Explore All Flavours (3D Product Cards):**
   - 3 interactive cards with rotating 3D mini cans:
     - **Natural Pure**
     - **Golden Pineapple**
     - **Ruby Watermelon**
   - Interactive "+" buttons that update cart count and trigger celebratory confetti.

7. **Section 6: Story & Polaroid Tilt Gallery:**
   - "YOU DESERVE THE BEST FOR YOUR BODY."
   - Realistic Polaroid photographs with realistic organic tilts, tape shadows, and hover straighten effects.

8. **Section 7: Reviews / Sippers Say:**
   - Horizontal slider with authentic quotes from the video ("I JUST LOVE THE PRODUCT", "TASTES LIKE A HOLIDAY", "MY MORNING RITUAL NOW", "CLEAN, CRISP, UNREAL").
   - Interactive next/prev navigation arrows.

9. **Section 8: Box of Health:**
   - Dual-card bundle showcase with 12-pack carton visual.

10. **Section 9: THE FRUIT NINJA GAME ("SLASH ACTIVATED"):**
    - The star feature highlighted in the video that clients loved!
    - Giant "PALMO" watermark with palm tree silhouettes.
    - Coconuts, watermelons, pineapples, strawberries, and limes fly up with real gravitational physics.
    - User drags or swipes to slash with a glowing laser/katana blade trail!
    - Fruits split into two realistic halves with juice splatter particles.
    - Live score counter, combos (`x2`, `x3`, `x5`), and milestone confetti!

11. **Section 10: MR NAEEM Branding, Credits & Hire Modal:**
    - Header & Footer branded with **MR NAEEM**.
    - "About MR Naeem" & "Hire MR Naeem" modal with direct WhatsApp chat link (`wa.me`) and inquiry form.
    - "View Tech Stack" breakdown modal.

12. **Web Audio API Sound Engine:**
    - Zero external mp3 dependencies.
    - High-fidelity synthesized sounds for blade whoosh, fruit splat, coconut crack, and bubble pops.
    - Sound toggle button (`SOUND ON / SOUND OFF`).

## 🚀 How to Run:

1. Simply double-click **`run.bat`** in this folder.
   OR
2. Open terminal in this folder and run:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   and visit `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
